Find answers to the most common inquiries about VeloQuote.

What is VeloQuote?

VeloQuote is an AI-powered platform that transforms raw T12s into HUD/GSE-loan friendly data. Upload your files, review AI-mapped categories, and export formula-intact Excel files ready for your sizer. What typically takes hours is completed in minutes.

Does VeloQuote replace my Excel sizer?

No. VeloQuote prepares and maps your operating statement data, then exports Excel files for use in your existing loan model.

What file formats does VeloQuote accept?

VeloQuote currently supports T12 operating statements in Excel. Direct PDF upload is coming soon. Sign up today for early access now.

How does VeloQuote’s category mapping work?

VeloQuote maps operating statement line items to HUD/GSE-loan friendly categories. You review the mapped data and flagged discrepancies before exporting to your model.

About VeloQuote

For over two decades and hundreds of HUD/GSE multifamily & healthcare screening and underwriting projects, VeloQuote founders Ernest B. & Rob F. have been trusted underwriting experts in institutional multifamily & healthcare lending.

They built VeloQuote because they lived the problem firsthand: hours lost to messy T12s, inconsistent category mapping, and manual data entry that slowed every deal. They knew the process could be faster without sacrificing the accuracy lenders depend on.

With VeloQuote’s AI-powered engine, their deep HUD/GSE multifamily & healthcare lending expertise is embedded directly into the technology, combining decades of institutional underwriting knowledge with advanced machine learning to deliver clean, lending-friendly data in minutes instead of hours.
